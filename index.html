Studio Philosophy

Atmosphere,
proportion and place.

Barkwood Design works between architectural clarity, localized craftsmanship and visualization. The aim is not simply to produce an image, but to build a recognizable spatial character that holds from concept through execution.

Residential design is a major strength of the studio, complemented by hospitality, commercial, corporate, mixed use, planning, landscape, sacred, healthcare and education work.
